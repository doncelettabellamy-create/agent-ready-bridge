Start with the customer. Remove everything in the way.

Every minute spent exporting a spreadsheet, searching an old system, or asking a colleague "what's on Thursday?" is a minute not spent on the person in front of you. We work backwards from that: what would you do if the answer were already there?

No migration

Keep the tools you have. Export to JSON and point the bridge at the file.

No new dashboard

Ask in plain language, inside the assistant you already use.

No risk to your data

Read-only and local. The bridge never writes to your records and makes no network calls.

Three steps. No project plan.

  1. Export your appointments, customers or orders to a JSON file.
  2. Point the bridge at it with BUSINESS_DATA_PATH, or use the included database.json.
  3. Ask your assistant: "Which appointments are still pending?"

Built on the open Model Context Protocol (MCP), so it works with Claude and with other MCP-capable clients.
